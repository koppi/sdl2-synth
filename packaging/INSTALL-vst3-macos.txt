SDL2 Synth - VST3 plug-in (macOS, Apple Silicon and Intel)
==========================================================

Copy the "sdl2-synth.vst3" bundle into one of the VST3 folders:

  per user:    ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
  system-wide: /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/

Then rescan plug-ins in your host. The plug-in appears as an instrument called
"SDL2 Synth". Requires macOS 11 or newer.

If the plug-in was not signed with an Apple Developer ID, macOS may refuse to
load it because it came from the internet ("cannot be opened because the
developer cannot be verified"). Remove the quarantine flag once after copying:

  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/sdl2-synth.vst3
